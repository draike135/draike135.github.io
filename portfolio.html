AI Accelerator on SoC-FPGA

Master Thesis · IST · 2025

Custom hardware accelerators for attention mechanisms and image preprocessing on a Xilinx SoC-FPGA. A hybrid hardware-software design offloads compute-heavy work to HLS IP cores, reaching a ~40× reduction in execution cycles via parallel accumulator architectures and 8-bit quantization.
